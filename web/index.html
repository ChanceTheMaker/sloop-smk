To install:

New in 2.8.0-b3: New NOTES and DRUMS editing, sequencer and arpeggiator updates, broader dual-core rendering, and blue/green/yellow/orange pads in pairs that turn white when hit. Pad brightness defaults to Low; valid saved settings take precedence. Original-firmware restore and flash compatibility fixes are retained. Release notes and test results.

Connect your SMK directly to the computer by USB and power it on. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.

Close Studio and other MIDI apps, confirm the version, then use the installer below. Do not unplug USB or switch off while writing.

Select your model and installation type. Official 016 firmware is downloaded automatically to prepare the model-specific package in your browser. Browser Studio and Simulator need no firmware installation. B2 adds guarded storage for projects, presets, settings and autosave. Existing Felucca saves are preserved but cannot be imported or overwritten. Sample preparation remains explicit; TRS routing is available in Settings.

First installation or coming from stock firmware?

Already running the SMK USB updater?

Felucca SMK 0.9.22+, Sloop SMK, Melodee SMK and updater-enabled Doom SMK can receive this portable update directly over a normal USB cable. Connect the SMK directly to the computer, power it on, and use Chrome or Edge.

Still on stock firmware or Test10?

For stock firmware, first use the official updater to install Pro 016 or Elite 016. Then choose Automatic install / resume below. Test10 and older private builds need a separate migration.

Release notes and source

  






Meet your new custom SMK-37 controls...


Choose a group to highlight the controls and learn what they do.


Knobs
KNOB BANK off
Bank 1 matches the FM-1: knob 1 master volume, 2 tempo, 3 factory preset, and 4 track.
KNOB BANK on
Bank 2: knob 1 track, 2 synth engine, 3 factory preset, and 4 page.
KNOBS 5–8
Edit the four parameter cards on the current screen in either bank.
Turn clockwise
Increase a value or move forward through choices.
Sound pages
PATCH / HOME
Return to the mix view and preset browsing. The < and > buttons browse factory sounds here.
PARA / SAVE
Open Projects without saving immediately. Knob 5 selects a slot; > saves and < loads.
FX
Cycle through effects, extra effects, and performance controls.
GLOBE
Tap to cycle through global controls, controls and Projects. Hold to open grouped navigation; K5 selects the family, K6 selects a page, and > opens it.
ARP / REPEAT
Toggle the arpeggiator or note repeat and open its controls.
SCALE / CHORD
Toggle scale snapping or chord playing and open the corresponding page.
BT / EDIT
Tap to cycle the synth editing pages, including FM6 operator pages for FM6 tracks. Hold for Settings; K5 chooses a setting, K6 changes it, and > saves. Bluetooth is not enabled in this build.
Play & record
SEQ
Cycle through sequencer setup, step editing, step detail, and automation.
PLAY
Start or stop playback. On Song Chain, start the arranged sections.
REC
Toggle recording for the selected track. Recording starts playback when stopped.
PADS / < >
Use pads for step entry on the step pages; the arrow buttons change the editing position.
Keep a take
Recording stays in memory until you save a project.
Keys & wheels
37 KEYS
Play the selected track with velocity-sensitive piano keys.
OCT− / OCT+
Shift the keyboard down or up an octave, within the three-octave shift range.
PITCH
Bend the note pitch; the wheel returns to its center position.
MOD
Apply modulation to the selected sound.
SUSTAIN
Connect a sustain pedal to hold notes after releasing keys. 
Pads & faders
16 PADS
Play the sixteen Sloop drum lanes with velocity.
PAD BANK
The first Sloop port uses one sixteen-pad drum bank.
FADER BANK off
The four faders control levels for tracks 1–4.
FADER BANK on
The four faders control attack, decay, sustain, and release for the selected track.
Illuminated buttons
Lights show active banks, playing modes, and transport state.
Save & arrange
PARA / SAVE
Open Sloop's native save pages for user presets and projects. B2 supports persistent saves in blank or Sloop-owned slots; existing Felucca slots are preserved and refused.
SONG
Arrange Sloop scenes and song sections using the native pages or Studio's Song tab.
BROWSER EXPORT
Export session keeps your browser sounds, projects, samples and MIDI settings in a local file.

Sloop, on your SMK


Native Sloop 2.8
Three synth parts and a dedicated drum track, native sound engines, six-operator FM, synth drums, parameter locks, microtiming, fill conditions, insert effects and texture.


SMK controls
37 velocity-sensitive keys, sixteen drum pads, eight encoders, four faders, pitch, mod and sustain. The display runs Sloop's own page and menu code.


Grouped Studio
Envelope, LFO, instrument, voice, effects, scale and arpeggiator controls stay together. Choose knobs or sliders and drag sections into your preferred order.


Hardware-style simulator
A playable SMK panel with color finishes, illuminated controls and the native firmware screen. Studio and Simulator share a browser session.


USB MIDI input
Play either browser view from a connected MIDI keyboard. Notes, velocity, sustain, pitch bend and modulation feed the native Sloop engine.


Keep browser sessions
Browser projects, presets and four virtual sample slots save locally and export to a session file. Physical-device storage is a separate porting step.
